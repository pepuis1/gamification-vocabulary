01 / EARN

Students earn 10 XP points after completing a learning activity.

Which game element is this?

That's right!

Not quite — here's why:

Points reward a completed action. Earning XP recognizes effort and can motivate students to keep participating.

02 / TRACK

Students can see that they have completed 75% of their online course.

Which game element is this?

That's right!

Not quite — here's why:

A progress indicator shows how much of a course is complete. Seeing 75% helps students track their progress toward a goal.

03 / DECIDE

Students can choose between creating a video or an infographic to demonstrate their understanding.

Which game element is this?

That's right!

Not quite — here's why:

Choice lets students select how to show what they know. Picking a video or infographic gives them ownership of their learning.

04 / LEARN

Students immediately see an explanation of why their answer was incorrect.

Which game element is this?

That's right!

Not quite — here's why:

Feedback explains the result of an action. An immediate explanation helps students understand and correct a mistake.

05 / GROW

Students complete increasingly difficult learning activities as they advance.

Which game element is this?

That's right!

Not quite — here's why:

Challenge asks students to tackle tasks that grow in difficulty. This helps them build skills as they advance.
